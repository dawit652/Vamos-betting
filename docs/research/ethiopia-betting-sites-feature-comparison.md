# Ethiopia Betting Sites: Feature Comparison

_Research snapshot: 7 October 2026. Companion to [ethiopia-sports-betting-market.md](./ethiopia-sports-betting-market.md), which covers regulation and payments in depth._

> **Context:** every Ethiopian sports betting licence was revoked on 15 Dec 2025, and no operator is licensed today. This document describes the features operators offered **before** the shutdown, plus what offshore sites still advertise, so the Vamos product can be benchmarked ahead of a relaunch. It is not a recommendation to use any of these sites.

---

## 1. Method and source quality

- Operator websites could not be opened from the research environment. Data comes from news outlets (Capital Ethiopia, Addis Fortune, Shega, The Reporter) and from betting review/affiliate sites.
- **Affiliate sites contradict each other a lot**, especially on minimum stakes, bonus percentages and app availability. Where sources disagree, this document shows the **range** reported.
- Legend used in the matrices:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Reported by several sources, consistent |
| ◐ | Reported by a single source, or sources conflict |
| ❌ | Reported as **not** available |
| — | No data found |

### Regulatory note on games

- The NLA (now ELS) licensed **sports betting only**. Virtual games were allowed only **as an add-on for licensed sports betting operators, on sports-related events**. In Dec 2022 it shut 20 shops for running unauthorised virtual games.
- One source says the NLA does not license other forms of gambling. Casino and crash games (Aviator, slots, Plinko) on local sites were therefore legally **grey at best**. Expect the relaunch rules to be stricter.

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses

### Local operators
**Strengths**
- low stakes
- multi-bonus ladders and jackpots
- local leagues
- mobile money as the main payment method
- works on low-end phones and 3G
- Amharic at some brands
- physical shop networks

**Weaknesses**
- opaque, inconsistent bonus terms
- limited cash-out markets
- few native iOS apps
- almost no live streaming or bet builders
- Afaan Oromo and Tigrinya not supported
- serious compliance failures: under-reported revenue, minors in shops

### International operators
**Strengths**
- market depth
- bet builders and multi-live
- full casinos
- polished apps
- crypto and many e-wallets

**Weaknesses**
- unlicensed and illegal for residents
- higher minimum deposits
- slower withdrawals at some brands
- weak local-league coverage and localisation
- huge bonuses with heavy wagering requirements

## 7. Gap analysis: what Vamos should offer at relaunch

### Must have (features every local leader offered)
1. Minimum stake around **5 ETB**. Low minimum deposit.
2. **telebirr + CBE Birr + M-Pesa** deposits and withdrawals to the registered wallet.
3. Football first, including the **Ethiopian Premier League**. Pre-match and **live** betting.
4. **Accumulator multi-bonus ladder.** This is Vamos's historic strength; keep it with **clear, published terms**.
5. **Weekly and daily jackpot** (13- and 9-fixture style).
6. **Cash-out** on main markets.
7. Fast **mobile web that works on 3G** plus a small Android APK.
8. **Amharic** + English.

### Differentiators (gaps nobody filled well)
1. **Transparent bonuses.** One plain-language terms page, the same everywhere. Competitors' terms contradict each other, which hurts trust.
2. **Guaranteed payout times** with a visible status tracker (pending → approved → paid) and SMS confirmation.
3. **Bet builder** and **wider cash-out** (partial cash-out, more markets). Today these are only offered offshore.
4. **Live match tracker / visualisation.** It works where streaming rights are too costly, and stats for local leagues should update quickly.
5. **More languages:** Afaan Oromo and Tigrinya. Nobody offers them.
6. **Native iOS app** or a high-quality PWA.
7. **SMS/USSD betting** for feature phones (Betika's model elsewhere in Africa), if the regulator allows it.
8. **Responsible-gambling tools** in the core product: 21+ age gate with Fayda ID, deposit/loss limits, cool-off, self-exclusion. This is likely to be a licensing requirement after the crackdown.

### Avoid or keep behind a licence check
- Casino, crash and slot games unless the new framework explicitly allows them.
- Virtual games beyond the sports-related scope the regulator permits.
- Crypto, agent/hawala channels, and payouts to third-party wallets.
- Uncapped or confusing bonus promotions.
