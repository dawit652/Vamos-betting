**Handing over?** The remaining tasks, ready to give to another session, are in [hulusport-parity-handover.md](hulusport-parity-handover.md).

**Nothing in this session changes what players or staff see by default.** Every new rule is either pure logic not yet wired to the money path, a measurement, a shadow recorder, or behind a switch that is off.

### Facts measured in this session

- **Catalogue size.** The first catalogue (515 selections) listed only 437–477 options after pruning; with task 2.8 (673 selections) the four reference matches list **635** (balanced, 1.45 v 1.15), **629** (away favourite), **596** (heavy home favourite) and **587** (low-scoring). The prototype measured 503–541 for its own catalogue.
- **Speed.** About 26 ms to price all 194 markets of one match on one core, with no grid trimming (Hulusport's board of 1,100 matches would take about 30 seconds).
- **Draw correction sign.** In the standard Dixon–Coles form used here (τ00 = 1 − λh·λa·ρ, τ11 = 1 − ρ), a **negative** ρ raises 0-0 and 1-1. Fitted values are usually about −0.1 to −0.2. Keep this in mind for the fit (P-4).
- **Equivalence.** Pricing today's nine market types through the rule table reproduces today's odds exactly. The non-void mass is summed in the same order as today's code, so whole-line Asian handicap and draw-no-bet prices do not move.

## History test of the families (check 0.6, 13 November)

Command: `pnpm --filter @vamos/scraper-app cli eval families --dir <folder>` on 36 football-data.co.uk files: Premier League, Championship, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1, seasons 2019/20 to 2024/25 (the seasons with closing prices), from `https://www.football-data.co.uk/mmz4281/<season>/<league>.csv`. **13,949 matches** priced (67 without a fit, 3 over the fit residual). What it says:

1. **The time-based split gives the first half too many goals.** The data's first-half share is 0.447. Second-half overs, "second half scores more" and first-half unders are under-priced by 6 to 17%. This is plan item P-5 (task 2.2, A11): `firstHalfShare` exists in the pricing and is off.
2. **Turnarounds between halves are under-priced by about 30%** (`htft` 2/1 and 1/2, `htcs_ft_result`). The halves are priced as independent with the same team strengths; in real matches the trailing side pushes, so comebacks are more common. A first-half share does not fix this: these three families need a large margin (the suggested 31–41%) or must stay off until the model joins the halves.
3. **With the first-half share switched on, 19 of the 23 pass.** `eval families --first-half-share 0.447` (the measured share) leaves **4 failures**, all turnarounds: `htft` (suggested 21%), `htft_ou` (31%), `htcs_ft_result` (18%) and `h1_h2_1x2` (15%; the only family that got worse, from 13%). The average hold over the families rises from 7.58% to 7.63%; no full-time family changes (the share only moves the halves). Adding the draw correction (`--draw-correction=-0.1`) changes nothing for the four and is mixed elsewhere (better for `goal_range`, worse for `exact_goals` and `odd_even`): **leave it off** until it is fitted.
4. **Recommendation for going live:** set `market_families.firstHalfShare` to 0.447 (task A11 added the setting), give the four turnaround families their suggested margins in `typeMarginBps` or leave them out, then store the test with `eval families --dir … --write` so the weekly job knows it.
